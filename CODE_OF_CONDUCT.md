# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

MLReproMutate is an open-source research software project. We want its community spaces to be professional, constructive, and welcoming to people with different backgrounds, levels of experience, identities, and perspectives.

Everyone who participates in the project is expected to treat others with respect and to contribute in good faith.

## Expected behavior

Examples of constructive participation include:

- communicating respectfully and professionally;
- giving technical criticism that is specific, evidence-based, and focused on the work rather than the person;
- accepting reasonable feedback and correcting mistakes when necessary;
- being considerate of differences in experience, language, culture, and communication style;
- respecting the boundaries, privacy, and time of other participants;
- helping keep discussions relevant to MLReproMutate, research-software quality, reproducibility, and project maintenance.

## Unacceptable behavior

The following are not acceptable in project spaces:

- harassment, intimidation, discrimination, or personal attacks;
- insulting, demeaning, threatening, or deliberately disruptive language;
- unwanted sexual attention or sexualized content directed at other participants;
- publishing another person's private information without permission;
- persistent off-topic disruption after a maintainer has asked that it stop;
- retaliation against someone who raises a concern in good faith;
- other conduct that a reasonable professional community would consider unsafe or abusive.

Disagreement about technical, methodological, or scientific questions is welcome. It should remain focused on evidence, reasoning, implementation, and reproducibility rather than on personal characteristics or motives.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to participation in MLReproMutate community spaces, including GitHub issues, pull requests, discussions, code review, documentation contributions, and other project-maintained communication channels.

It also applies when someone is explicitly representing MLReproMutate in a project-related public setting.

## Reporting a concern

Routine project questions, bugs, and feature requests should use the public GitHub issue tracker.

If a concern involves harassment, abuse, privacy, retaliation, or another matter that should not be reported publicly, contact the project maintainer through the private contact method listed on the maintainer's GitHub profile rather than opening a public issue.

Reports should include enough information to understand what happened, while avoiding unnecessary disclosure of private information.

## Enforcement

The project maintainer is responsible for interpreting and enforcing this Code of Conduct in project spaces.

Depending on the seriousness and pattern of behavior, responses may include:

1. a private or public reminder of expected conduct;
2. a request to edit, remove, or stop problematic behavior;
3. temporary limits on participation;
4. removal of comments, issues, pull requests, or other contributions that violate this policy;
5. permanent exclusion from project-maintained community spaces for severe or repeated violations.

Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, documented when appropriate, and made with attention to the safety and privacy of affected participants.

Reports made in good faith will not result in retaliation by project maintainers.
